About Us - Collab

Collab is a real-time communication platform powered by WebRTC and mediasoup, designed to deliver simple, secure, and fast video calls, messaging, and screen sharing capabilities in your browser.

Our mission is to create a seamless and robust communication platform that meets the needs of teams, educators, and individuals who rely on video collaboration.

We offer a fully self-hosted solution with no third-party servers required, giving you full control over your data. Whether for meetings, video conferences, or personal chats, Collab ensures your communication is secure and uninterrupted.
