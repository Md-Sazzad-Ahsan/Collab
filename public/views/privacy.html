Privacy Policy - Collab

At Collab, we value your privacy. We collect minimal data, such as your email and password, to create and authenticate your account. Your email is used solely for notifications like password resets.

We do not store sensitive information such as payment details or conversations. If you record a meeting, it is stored securely in your browser, then downloaded directly to your device.

Security Promise: Your data is encrypted, and we are committed to protecting your privacy. If we offer subscriptions in the future, payment information will be securely processed and stored with the utmost care.
